spoofy ☺

Experimental beta · iOS 17.0 RootHide
Device testing is pending. spoofy changes what one iOS-version API reports in the App Store. We do not know whether the Store uses this API for eligibility or whether the settings switch works on your device.
This is a display experiment. It does not install updates, bypass the installer, or supply newer iOS frameworks or APIs. Even if an app installs, it may not run.

Try it safely

After installation, open Settings → spoofy (Experimental). The switch starts off. Turn it on and close and reopen the App Store. Compare the ChatGPT listing; do not tap Update during this test.

To disable it, turn the switch off and reopen the App Store. Before uninstalling through Sileo, turn the switch off: preferences may persist after removal and take effect again after reinstalling. RootHide injection for Settings and App Store may need enabling, and access to the shared preference file remains unverified.

Existing ChatGPT version and data are not modified by this experiment. Source and test notes
