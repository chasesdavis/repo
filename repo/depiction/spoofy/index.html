spoofy ☺

Experimental beta · iOS 17.0 RootHide
Device testing is pending. spoofy changes what one iOS-version API reports in the App Store. We do not know whether the Store uses this API for eligibility or whether the settings switch works on your device.
This is a display experiment. It does not install updates, bypass the installer, or supply newer iOS frameworks or APIs. Even if an app installs, it may not run.

Try it safely

With the App Store closed, open Settings → spoofy (Experimental), choose Clear diagnostic report, then turn on Enabled and Record App Store status. Reopen the App Store and view the listing without tapping Update. Return to Settings → spoofy to read the status, time, and three call indicators.

The report contains a fixed status, timestamp, and three yes/no indicators. These may include calls from any code in the App Store, including diagnostic code; a call does not prove why it happened. No report, an old report, or an unrecorded call is inconclusive. Phone behavior remains unverified.

To stop, turn both switches off and reopen the App Store. The last report remains until cleared; close App Store before using Clear diagnostic report. Before uninstalling through Sileo, turn the switches off, because preferences may persist after removal. RootHide injection for Settings and App Store may need enabling.
