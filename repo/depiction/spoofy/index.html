Experimental beta 6 · iOS 17.0 RootHide
Beta 6 has not been tested on a phone. This experiment does not install updates, bypass the installer, or provide newer iOS frameworks or APIs. It does not establish app compatibility.

Preserve your report

Close the App Store and turn off spoofy’s switches before updating. The existing report is designed to remain until you explicitly clear it; take a screenshot before clearing it. Beta 6 settings and report preservation have not been verified on-device.

Optional diagnostic

To record a fresh test, close the App Store, preserve then clear the previous report, and enable Record App Store status and Record call context. Reopen the App Store, view a listing without installing or updating, then read the report in Settings → spoofy. The original Enabled switch controls the separate display experiment.

Call context records fixed yes/no flags for work marked as spoofy’s on the same thread and for unclassified calls. Unclassified does not identify a caller or prove an eligibility decision. No caller addresses, paths, arguments, account information, or app data are recorded.

To stop, turn all spoofy switches off and close and reopen the App Store. The report remains until cleared. Settings and App Store injection depend on your RootHide setup.

Keep existing apps and their data unchanged during this test. Source and test notes
