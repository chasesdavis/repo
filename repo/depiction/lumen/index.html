Lumen

Chase Davis · iOS 15 – 17 · roothide

Free, no subscription

What it does

Lumen turns the status bar clock into a small pill. It shows one live value at a time and a tap opens a card with the detail, without leaving the app you are in.

  • Time, date and battery
  • Now playing, with transport controls in the card
  • Network throughput and free storage
  • Countdown to a date you choose
  • Quick message composer from anywhere

How to use it

  • Long press the pill to cycle through your enabled pills
  • Tap the pill to open its card
  • Pick which pills exist and tune opacity, color and text size in Settings → Lumen
  • Changes apply instantly, no respring

Compatibility

  • iPhone and iPad, iOS 15.0 through 17.x
  • Dopamine roothide and rootful ElleKit setups
  • arm64 and arm64e
  • No dependencies beyond ElleKit and PreferenceLoader
